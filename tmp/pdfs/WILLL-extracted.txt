1. PROJECT PURPOSE 
Build an AI-powered personal safety application that helps protect people, particularly 
women and children, when travelling alone. 
Traditional safety applications rely on users pressing an SOS button. This application must 
additionally detect potential distress automatically and activate configured safety 
protocols without requiring manual intervention. 
The application must support: 
• Personalized emergency trigger phrases entered during registration. 
• Passive detection of screams, shouting, and distress-related vocal sounds. 
• Voice-based distress detection using machine learning. 
• Movement-based safety signals where appropriate. 
• An agentic AI safety decision engine. 
• Continuous live location tracking during active journeys. 
• Route deviation detection. 
• Automatic emergency communication. 
• Live location sharing with authorized contacts. 
• Audio and video evidence capture when permitted. 
• A family and guardian monitoring dashboard. 
• Optional wearable integration in a later phase. 
Do not remove, weaken, or replace these requirements with a basic SOS-only application.

How Your Compulsory Features Will Be Implemented 
A. Personalized trigger words 
During registration, the user enters their own emergency phrases. 
For example: 
• "Help me now." 
• "Call my family." 
• A personalized secret phrase. 
The app stores the configuration and connects it to an offline-capable speech-recognition 
or keyword-spotting engine. 
When a configured emergency phrase is recognized, the app can activate the emergency 
protocol automatically. 
B. Automatic scream and distress detection 
The app processes microphone input through an audio classification model running on the 
phone. 
It evaluates sounds such as screaming, shouting, and other supported distress-related 
vocalizations. 
The detection engine passes its results to the safety agent, which decides whether to 
activate the configured emergency response. 
The sound detector and speech recognizer are separate components: recognizing a 
spoken phrase is different from classifying a scream. 
C. Always-on monitoring 

===== PAGE 33 =====
Kotlin services will handle the Android functionality that React Native alone cannot reliably 
guarantee, including background microphone and location operations. 
The app will use the appropriate permissions, foreground services, and notifications. 
Monitoring can continue while the screen is off when Android allows the service to operate. 
Force-stops, permission revocation, battery depletion, and operating-system restrictions 
can interrupt it. 
D. Live location tracking 
The app will use Android location services to obtain real coordinates. 
During an active journey, the backend receives location updates and the family dashboard 
displays the changing position. 
During an emergency, the app attempts to maintain more frequent updates and shares a 
link to the live tracking page with authorized contacts. 
A location link must show the latest available position and refresh as new locations arrive; 
it must not simply display a fixed coordinate. 
E. Automatic emergency alerts 
Firebase Cloud Functions will coordinate emergency processing and securely call the 
configured SMS and email providers. 
Firebase Cloud Messaging will handle supported push notifications. 
The system will record notification attempts, handle failures, and retry where appropriate. 
Actual SMS and email delivery requires configured provider accounts and valid credentials. 
F. Evidence recording 
Kotlin will handle the relevant Android recording integrations. 
When an emergency occurs, the app will attempt to capture permitted audio or video and 
associate it with the emergency event.
